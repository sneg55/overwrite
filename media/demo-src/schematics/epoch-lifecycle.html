One epoch

Each epoch is a full option lifecycle, and every step is a real state change on the ledger driven by a Daml choice. Mainnet epochs are weekly; demo epochs are compressed to minutes, which is a parameter, not a shortcut.

Open deposits
Vault.OpenDeposits, Vault.Deposit
CBTC moves into the pool. Each depositor holds one VaultPosition for the epoch, topped up if they deposit again while the window is open.
→
Lock collateral
Vault.LockCollateral
The pool is locked as a CIP-56 allocation, the token standard's own lock and DvP primitive, never a bespoke Daml lock.
→
Write the call
Vault.WriteCall, PayPremium
A CallOption co-signed by the vault and the buyer. The buyer pays the premium on-ledger, against the locked collateral.
→
Pay the holders
VaultPosition.PayoutPremium
One real transfer per holder, each with its own PremiumReceipt. Premium is pushed out, never accrued as a claimable balance.
↓
At expiry
Settlement reads a PriceObservation contract by id, keyed by asset and epoch, with freshness and wrong-epoch guards. No price is passed in as an argument.
PRICE AT OR BELOW STRIKE
Out of the money
The allocation is withdrawn and the collateral returns to the pool. Holders keep the premium.
CallOption.SettleOTM
PRICE ABOVE STRIKE
In the money
Atomic physical delivery: the locked CBTC and the cash strike leg move in a single transaction. No escrow contract risk, no public-mempool MEV.
CallOption.SettleITM
↓
Record the epoch
Vault.RecordEpoch
An EpochReport per depositor carrying aggregate results only: settlement path, totals, depositor count. Never a per-holder payout list.
→
Roll
Vault.RollPositions
Positions carry into the next epoch unless a withdrawal was queued, in which case principal is returned at the boundary.

The market maker and the price oracle are SIMULATED and labeled everywhere they appear. Premium figures are demo parameters, not market pricing.
